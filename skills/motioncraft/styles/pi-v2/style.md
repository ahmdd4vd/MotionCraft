# pi-v2

Clean, bright and airy. A blue-white background with two slow soft glows, dark ink text, and one blue accent word per sentence. Words rise out of a light blur exactly when they are spoken. Real lit 3D objects (soft glossy shapes, a mascot, 3D logos) add depth without noise. Scenes blur-crossfade into each other while the camera drifts slowly, so the frame is never frozen.

**Fonts:** Figtree (display), JetBrains Mono (code). **Music:** `dreamy`. **SFX:** `soft-pop`.

## Do
- Keep lots of empty space; one headline and one hero per frame.
- Use UI cards, checklists, counters and step cards as proof.
- Land the big reveal on the music drop.

## Don't
- Add colored blobs, extra gradients or more accent colors.
- Use emoji as icons or flat CSS "3D".
- Put more than 2 lines of text on screen.
