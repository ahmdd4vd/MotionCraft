# warm-paper

Warm off-white paper with a peach glow, dark brown ink, and a terracotta accent. Rounded friendly type (Manrope) and slightly softer shadows. Feels human and handmade, not techy.

**Fonts:** Manrope, IBM Plex Mono. **Music:** `lofi-desk`. **SFX:** `paper`.

## Do
- Use for stories, lifestyle, food, learning and personal content.
- Pair with photos or hand-drawn style icons.

## Don't
- Mix in cold blue UI elements.
- Use fast, punchy motion; keep energy around 0.4.
