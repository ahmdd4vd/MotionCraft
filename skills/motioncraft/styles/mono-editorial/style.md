# mono-editorial

Black and white with a magazine feel. Serif headlines (Newsreader), thin borders, flat cards, almost no shadow, and one deep red spot color for the accent word. Motion is calm: less blur, little camera drift, small tilt.

**Fonts:** Newsreader, IBM Plex Mono. **Music:** `cinematic-soft`. **SFX:** `paper`.

## Do
- Use for opinion, essays, news-style explainers, quotes.
- Let big serif headlines carry the frame; fewer objects.

## Don't
- Add colored 3D shapes or bright UI.
- Use the red for anything except the one accent word.
