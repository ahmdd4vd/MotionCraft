# tech-neon-soft

Dark indigo with soft mint and violet light. Space Grotesk headlines, mint accent, dark glassy cards. Techy and modern without cyberpunk noise.

**Fonts:** Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono. **Music:** `tech-bright`. **SFX:** `digital-soft`.

## Do
- Use for dev tools, AI products, launches, changelogs.
- Keep glows low and blurred; let the accent do the work.

## Don't
- Add scanlines, glitch effects or hard neon outlines.
- Use more than one of mint / violet as text color in the same frame.
