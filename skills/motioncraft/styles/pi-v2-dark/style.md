# pi-v2-dark

pi-v2 at night. Near-black blue background with dim blue glows, soft light text, and one bright blue accent. Same motion, same layout rules - it just feels more focused and premium.

**Fonts:** Figtree, JetBrains Mono. **Music:** `dark-minimal`. **SFX:** `glass`.

## Do
- Use for dev tools, security, late-night or "serious" topics.
- Keep cards dark (`card` token) with a thin light border.

## Don't
- Push the glows brighter - they turn into neon.
- Use pure white for big blocks; it glares on dark.
